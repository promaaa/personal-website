I

Machines, systems & prototypes.

Five builds, each argued in theory and then tested against the physical world. The margin carries the apparatus.

  1. Autonomous ball-collecting robot

    Optical-bearing guidance, first-order identification of the DC motor’s transfer function, and cascaded PI loops on speed and heading, for positioning within ±0.5–1 cm.

    Robotics, PID control, motor identification.

    Source code of the ball-collecting robot

  2. SmartChess

    An end-to-end pipeline: a 64-reed sensor matrix, PyTorch CNN corner tracking, and an embedded engine, IA-Marc V2, running on a Raspberry Pi 5. Build photographs and CAD included.

    Embedded systems, computer vision, a chess engine.

    Source code of SmartChess · Film of SmartChess

  3. Nand2cpu, a 16-bit ALU

    An arithmetic logic unit built from first principles in Verilog, with an automated verification testbench (7 + 8 = 15), an assembler, and a recorded demonstration.

    Digital logic in Verilog.

    Source code of Nand2cpu · Film of Nand2cpu

  4. OAI 5G CU–DU split

    An OpenAirInterface split DU on Jetson Orin Nano and Raspberry Pi 5 for lightweight UAV cells, over Ethernet, Wi-Fi and 5G backhaul. Paper submitted, 2026.

    5G NR, OpenAirInterface, NVIDIA Jetson.

    Source code of the 5G testbed · Paper on the 5G CU-DU split (PDF)

  5. Evento

    Wallet-based ticketing on Solana Pay, built under hackathon constraints, with a clean boundary between on-chain and off-chain state and sub-second finality.

    Solana Pay and Django.

    Source code of Evento

Select bibliography

  • Dostoevsky, Fyodor. The Brothers Karamazov. 1880. The Grand Inquisitor alone is worth the voyage.
  • Dumas, Alexandre. Le Comte de Monte-Cristo [The Count of Monte Cristo]. 1844. A masterclass in composure: playing the twenty-year game.
  • Frankl, Viktor E. Man’s Search for Meaning. 1946. A “why” that outlasts any collapse of the “how”.
  • Gary, Romain. La Promesse de l’aube [Promise at Dawn]. 1960. Put it down, and call your mother.
  • Gombrich, E. H. The Story of Art. 1950. The best companion before any museum.

  • Herman, Edward S., & Noam Chomsky. Manufacturing Consent. 1988. Five filters, and a firewall against manufactured consensus.

    ‡ On those who publish past the five filters, the editor’s copy reads: redacted: Courage is contagious.

  • La Boétie, Étienne de. Discours de la servitude volontaire [Discourse on Voluntary Servitude]. 1576. Tyrants hold only the power we consent to give.
  • Rand, Ayn. Atlas Shrugged. 1957. The mind as the prime mover.
  • Snowden, Edward. Permanent Record. 2019. Maintain your tools, or they will maintain you.
  • Verne, Jules. Le Tour du monde en quatre-vingts jours [Around the World in Eighty Days]. 1872. Reread before the GR20.
