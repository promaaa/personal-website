Sailing directions

Built through theory. Tested in practice.

A robotics and embedded systems engineer, now a master’s student in the 3D Vision Lab at Seoul National University. Before that, a research intern at KAUST, in the SeRBER lab, working on efficient vision models, NVIDIA Jetson deployment and a 5G testbed for UAV relay cells.

The background spans autonomous robotics, closed-loop control, custom PCB layout and real-time firmware: turning complex physical problems into deterministic hardware with clean software abstractions.

Equipment aboard. STM32 and ESP32; embedded C and C++; FreeRTOS; Altium and KiCad; Verilog and FPGA; CAN, SPI and I²C; PID control and kinematics; OpenCV; AI and robotics.
